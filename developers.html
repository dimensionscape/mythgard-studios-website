Meet the Team Behind the Magic

Mythgard Studios is a passion-driven indie team of developers and designers creating imaginative 2D worlds fueled by our love of storytelling, pixel art, and the Godot engine.

Our Origin Story

Mythgard Studios began with a dream shared by friends and gamers. After years of immersion in fantasy worlds, we decided it was time to build our own. With a focus on deeply immersive gameplay, Norse-inspired mythology, and handcrafted pixel visuals—we set out to bring Valerune to life using the powerful, open-source Godot Engine.

We're not a big team, we are passionate creators wearing many hats, collaborating every day to make something meaningful. Together, we represent both sides of the forge: design and development, heart and hands.
