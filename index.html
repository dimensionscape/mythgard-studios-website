Who We Are

Mythgard Studios is a collective of lifelong gamers turned developers, bound together by a love of immersive worlds, handcrafted pixel art, and player-first design. We specialize in 2D experiences with depth—combining life-sim, RPG, and narrative-driven gameplay mechanics to forge something new, yet rooted in classic inspiration.

Our journey began not in a boardroom, but in bedrooms and Discord servers—where passion met code, and ideas turned into pixels. We proudly build our worlds in the open-source Godot Engine, believing in creative freedom, community support, and full ownership of our craft.

Every game we create is a love letter to the genres we grew up with—crafted not for trends, but for players who crave meaning behind the mechanics.

What Drives Us

At Mythgard Studios, we’re not chasing mainstream— we’re building mythologies. Our development philosophy blends emotional storytelling with systems-rich gameplay. We don’t just want to entertain; we want to make you *feel* something.
